Adult Admissions (>16 years)

General Principles

  • • Face to Face: All referrals require face-to-face review by the specialty.
  • • Admitting Specialty: Defined by the acute pathology requiring admission.
  • • Disputes: ED Consultant makes the final decision on admission.

Conflict Resolution

Advice on medical management can be sought from RMO1 or Acute Med Consultant. Once referred, onward referral is the specialty's responsibility.

Medicine

  • Chest Pain
  • Cardiac Arrhythmia / Palpitations
  • Shortness of Breath
  • Haemoptysis
  • Haematemesis / Melaena
  • Seizures
  • Headache
  • Dysphagia
  • Lower & Upper Limb DVT
  • DKA / Hypoglycaemia
  • Non-Specific Deterioration / Frailty Syndrome
  • Overdose requiring admission
  • Renal Failure (NOT obstructive/outflow)
  • Painless Jaundice
  • Chronic Pancreatitis
  • Lower Limb Cellulitis
  • Loin Pain (UTI / Uncomplicated Pyelonephritis)
  • Pubic Rami Uncomplicated
  • Anaemia (no active bleeding)
  • Dialysis patients with medical conditions
  • SOL / Spontaneous SAH (awaiting NORSE)
  • Infection of unclear origin
  • Haem/Onc complications (unless surgical e.g. obstruction)

General Surgery

  • Acute Abdominal Pain
  • Acute Pancreatitis / Cholecystitis
  • Abdominal Trauma
  • Loin Pain (Urine clear, no stones/AAA)
  • Abscess (Trunk, Perineum, Groin)
  • Fresh PR Bleeding
  • Painful Jaundice
  • Suspected Necrotising Fasciitis (Follow pathway)
  • Isolated Chest Injury / Rib # / Pneumothorax
  • Polytrauma (Chest/Abdo primary)

Urology

  • Loin pain (Proven/recent stone)
  • Urinary Retention
  • Visible Haematuria
  • Fournier's Gangrene (Nec Fasc genitalia)
  • Complicated Pyelonephritis (Collections/emphysema)
  • Obstructive Uropathy / Bladder Outflow Obstruction

Vascular

  • Acute Ischaemic Limb
  • Chronic Limb Ischaemia
  • AAA (Bleeding, Ruptured, Dissecting)
  • Diabetic Foot leading to sepsis

Trauma & Orthopaedics

  • Limb / Pelvic / Spinal Injuries (Primary reason)
  • Suspected Cauda Equina (NOT MSCC)
  • Acute Limb Pain
  • Abscess (Upper & Lower Limbs)
  • Upper Limb Cellulitis (inc. Hand Infections)
  • Osteomyelitis (Upper or Lower limb)
  • Polytrauma (Limb/Pelvic primary)
Exceptions (Admit to Medicine):
Uncomplicated pubic rami fractures.
Isolated vertebral fragility fractures.

Head Injuries

Manage according to NICE Guidelines.

Primary Head Injury:

Make NORSE Referral for acute advice (transfer or local management).

Exception (Admit to Medicine):

Frail adult, decision NOT for neurosurgical intervention (futility).

Gynaecology

  • Suspected Ectopic (Haemodynamic compromise)
  • Suspected Miscarriage (Heavy bleed + compromise)
  • Suspected Ovarian Torsion
  • Heavy Vaginal Bleeding
  • PID (Severe or suspected abscess)
  • Bartholin's Abscess
  • Complications of Gynae Surgery
Direct to Gynae CNS (via GAU):
Early pregnancy (<16wks) who are clinically stable (Ectopic/Miscarriage/Hyperemesis).
GP Urgent Reviews:
Direct to GAU if stable.

Maternity (>16 wks)

  • Obstetric Emergency (Bleeding, Pain, Pre-eclampsia)
Patient stable? → Direct to MUAU
CRITICAL (Imminent birth, Eclampsia, Arrest):
Treat in ED + Call "Obstetric Emergency" (2222).

ENT

  • Epistaxis
  • Stridor
  • Tonsillitis / Quinsy
  • ENT Malignancy
  • Head & Neck Soft Tissue Infection
  • Alimentary Foreign Body (Above sternal notch)
